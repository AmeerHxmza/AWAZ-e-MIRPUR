Mirpur City AJK Municipal & Water Regulations:
1. All drinking water pipeline leakages and supply shortages must be reported to the Public Health Engineering Department / Water Board Mirpur AJK.
2. Sewage blockages and drain overflows fall under Municipal Corporation Mirpur (MCM) with a standard response SLA of 48 hours.
3. Road damage, potholes, and unauthorized encroachments are under the jurisdiction of the Mirpur Development Authority (MDA) and MCM Works Department.
4. Waste dumping in open public areas or near Mangla Dam catchments is strictly penalized under AJK Local Government Environmental Bylaws.
5. Official contact: info@mirpur.ajk.gov.pk / complaints@mcm.ajk.gov.pk
